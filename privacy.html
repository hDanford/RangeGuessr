Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

RangeGuessr is designed to respect your privacy. This page explains what data the game touches and why. In plain terms: the game itself does not run accounts, does not ask for personal information, and stores your progress on your own device.

Data stored on your device

Your game history, scores, and streak are saved in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is used only to show your stats, calendar, and streak. Clearing your browser data — or playing in a different browser or device — will reset this progress. We have no way to recover it because we never receive it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, location, or any account credentials. We do not build user profiles or sell data, because we don't gather it in the first place.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand how many people play and which features are used. Any such measurement is reported in aggregate and is not used to identify individual players.

Advertising

RangeGuessr may display advertising to help cover its costs. If served through Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can manage personalized advertising via Google Ad Settings. Third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits; you may opt out of personalized advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance.

Cookies

The game's own functionality relies on local storage rather than tracking cookies. Any cookies present are most likely set by the advertising or analytics services described above.

Children

RangeGuessr is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

External links and data sources

The game links to outside resources such as GBIF, the IUCN Red List, and conservation organizations. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed on the site.
