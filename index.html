🌿 How to Play

One guess

Each day a new animal is revealed. You get one chance to do two things:

  • Click the map — place a pin where you think the animal lives in the wild
  • Pick a conservation status — guess its IUCN Red List category

Then hit Submit. The range is revealed and you see your score.

Scoring — 10,000 points total

  • Location (5,000 pts): Click inside the range = 5,000. Further away = fewer points, decaying over ~2,000 km.
  • Conservation (5,000 pts): Exact match = 5,000. Each IUCN level off costs 1,000 pts.

Conservation status levels

CRCritically Endangered — extremely high risk
ENEndangered — high risk of extinction
VUVulnerable — high risk if threats continue
NTNear Threatened — close to qualifying
LCLeast Concern — widespread and stable

Daily streak

Play every day to build your streak. Any score counts — even a low one keeps the streak alive.

Free Play

Unlimited animals in random order. Score accumulates for your session but doesn't affect your daily streak.
